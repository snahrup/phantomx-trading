**Pattern:** holding-losers
**Evidence:** GUN/USDT session 2/13/2026 2:05-2:29 PM — 19 consecutive hold decisions during unbroken waterfall selloff. Every analysis tick confirmed 'no reversal signal' yet position was held until the daily loss limit kill switch forced an exit.
**Impact:** Hit daily loss limit — maximum allowed daily drawdown consumed on a single trade that had no structural reason to reverse
**Recommendation:** If 3+ consecutive analysis ticks show 'no reversal signal' during a waterfall decline, cut the position. Don't wait for the kill switch to do your job. Set a hard rule: 3 ticks of 'hold with no reversal' = manual exit.
