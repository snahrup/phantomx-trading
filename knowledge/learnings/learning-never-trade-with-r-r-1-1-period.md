The key takeaway is NON-NEGOTIABLE: I will never again enter a trade where my risk exceeds my reward. Even if I'm 80% confident, a 0.26:1 R:R means I need to win 79% of the time just to break even after fees. That's not sustainable. From now on: (1) Calculate R:R BEFORE entering, (2) If R:R < 1.5:1, I need an exceptionally strong edge (90%+ confidence), (3) If stop is wide, target must be proportionally wider — no asymmetric risk. This win was luck, not skill, and I refuse to repeat it.
