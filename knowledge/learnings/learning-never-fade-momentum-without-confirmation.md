The key takeaway: STOP shorting just because price 'looks high.' I need at least TWO confirmations before fading a trend: (1) momentum divergence or volume drop, and (2) structural break (breakdown below support or failed higher high). If I can't point to a specific signal beyond 'this feels toppy,' I don't have a trade. Also, never enter with R:R < 1.5:1 — if the risk/reward isn't compelling, the setup isn't ready. My job is to wait for the pitch, not swing at everything.
