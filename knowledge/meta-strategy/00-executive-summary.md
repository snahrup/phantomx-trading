# Meta-Strategy Research — Executive Summary

## Mission

Research the optimal agent roster and organizational structure for PhantomX's automated 50x leverage crypto trading operation on Phemex.

---

## Current Status (Heartbeat 7 — March 8, 2026 00:30 UTC)

### Phase 1 Complete: Roster Expansion Executed

The CEO implemented 75% of Phase 1-3 recommendations in a single heartbeat cycle:
- **Roster**: 10 → 17 agents (+70%)
- **Structure**: Flat → 2-level hierarchy (Head of Research, Head of Trading)
- **CEO span**: 9 → 7 direct reports
- **All 6 recommended new agents hired**: Backtester, On-Chain Analyst, Sentiment Analyst, Microstructure Analyst, Head of Research, Head of Trading
- **Bonus**: Trade Analyst added (standalone, better than my recommendation to enhance Portfolio Manager)

### Phase 2 Now: Coordination Infrastructure

The focus has shifted from "what agents do we need?" to "how do we make them work together effectively?"

**Remaining critical gaps (5 items blocking autonomous mode):**
1. Signal interchange format (agents speak different data languages)
2. Regime schema standardization (agents disagree on what "risk-off" means)
3. Backtester validates all strategies (agent exists, hasn't run yet)
4. Correlation monitoring in Risk Officer (uncorrelated risk = survival at 50x)
5. Paper trading trial (≥10 simulated trades)

**Risk management gaps (from 2026 AI trading research):**
- We have 1/5 recommended risk layers (kill switch only)
- Missing: volatility filter, correlation limits, regime-based sizing, adaptive sizing
- Crash correlation convergence makes multi-asset exposure far riskier than it appears

### From Heartbeat 7 (New Research)
8. **Architecture validated**: TradingAgents (UCLA/MIT) and QuantAgent (Stony Brook) use nearly identical agent structures. Our design is sound.
9. **Missing dialectical debate**: Head of Research should argue Bull vs Bear cases, not just synthesize. +25% improvement in testing.
10. **Disagreement = reduce size**: When agents conflict, reduce position size. Don't try to resolve — treat disagreement as risk.
11. **Funding rate carry is our best edge**: 15-35% annualized, structural, works regardless of direction. Should go live first.
12. **Crash correlations spike to 1.0**: During crashes, all crypto moves together. 50x on 3 assets ≈ 150x effective exposure.

## Bottom Line

The roster is built. The structure is sound. The agents are producing quality output.

**What's left before autonomous trading:**

2. **Add risk layers** — Volatility filter, correlation limits, adaptive sizing (we have 1/5)
3. **Validate strategies** — Backtester must run on all 4 strategies with formal pass/fail
4. **Prove it works** — Paper trading trial with full pipeline
5. **Board approval** — Steve switches mode from manual → autonomous
